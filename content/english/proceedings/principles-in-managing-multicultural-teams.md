Global Teams have become the norm. This session reviews Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions of a Team model and discusses how different cultures may react to each behavioral principle. Where a principle goes against some cultural norms, such as encouraging conflict, alternative approaches are explored, drawing on Erin Meyer's The Culture Map.
