A team from Jesus Film Project, Seed Company, SIL, and Wycliffe developed a translation process using video scenes from the Bible and oral drafting narrated alongside them. This presentation demonstrates two story-narrated videos, discusses story/scene selection, and presents lessons learned and opportunities for this approach with unreached people groups.
