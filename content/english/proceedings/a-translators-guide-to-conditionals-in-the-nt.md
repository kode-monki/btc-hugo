There are approximately 650 conditional sentences in the New Testament, and translating these can pose numerous challenges. Greek conditional sentences express a wide range of meanings including hypothetical, predictive, counterfactual, and factual conditionals. This paper describes a resource being prepared by a team at the Canada Institute of Linguistics to help translators navigate these challenges.
