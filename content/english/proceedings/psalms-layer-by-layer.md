A new multi-organisational initiative to produce innovative exegetical resources for the Psalms (Cambridge Digital Bible Research). The Psalms: Layer by Layer project displays the inner logic of OT Hebrew poetry through visual and prose components so that translators, songwriters, and pastors are equipped to understand the text and convey the message.
