A research team for the 'Psalms: Layer by Layer' resource investigated the needs of its intended users through a repeatable process based on user experience research. They interviewed 13 individuals of varied backgrounds, exploring current workflows and desires for content, organization, and formatting of new resources for translating OT poetry.
