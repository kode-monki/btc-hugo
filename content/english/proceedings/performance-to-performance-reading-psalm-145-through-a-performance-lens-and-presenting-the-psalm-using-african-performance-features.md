Psalm 145 has been described as both 'an example of sophisticated poetic art' and as 'poetically worthless.' Reading the Hebrew text as performance literature reveals a compelling responsorial structure. This paper examines the call-response format as used in West and South Africa and presents an empirical study with South African youth performing the psalm.
