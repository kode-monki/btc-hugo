Cross-cultural teams working in Bible translation face many obstacles including cultural differences in leadership style and theological differences. This paper addresses the need for spiritually mature leadership that can cross cultural boundaries and promote unity, and provides a framework for developing a communication strategy in cross-cultural leadership.
