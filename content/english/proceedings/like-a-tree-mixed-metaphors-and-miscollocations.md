The healthy tree image is frequent in OT poetry (Ps 1; 52; 92; Dan 4; Ezk 31; Hos 14). Several texts leave unclear where the referent ends and the image begins, resulting in strange collocations in Hebrew and in translation. This paper examines these texts using Ancient Near Eastern imagery and Hebrew poetic forms and offers suggestions for equivalent translation.
