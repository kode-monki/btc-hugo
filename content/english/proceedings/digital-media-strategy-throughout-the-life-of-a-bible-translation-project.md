In the last ten years in the USA, mobile phone use has risen from 45 minutes a day to over four hours. This presentation discusses how to use digital media strategically throughout a Bible translation project, with ideas that can be incorporated into current language projects as well as new projects, with a view to creating a sustainable and holistic digital media strategy.
