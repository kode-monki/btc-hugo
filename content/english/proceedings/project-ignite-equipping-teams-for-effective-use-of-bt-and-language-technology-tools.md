A partnership of Seed Company and the EMDC.online team, Project Ignite has trained over 1000 participants from 60 countries in tools such as Bloom, Paratext, and Logos. This presentation describes the program and explores how live virtual training is proving not merely a temporary solution to the pandemic but a new paradigm of training.
