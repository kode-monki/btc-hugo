<p>Matthew is an Assistant Professor of Missions and Theology at Cedarville University. Prior to serving at CU, he and his family lived in North Africa and the Middle East where he grew acquainted with Islam. In 20217 he earned his PhD, having researched the concept of Atonement within Islam and comparing it to the book of Hebrews.</p>
