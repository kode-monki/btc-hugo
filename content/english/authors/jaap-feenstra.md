Jaap Feenstra grew up in a bilingual community in the Netherlands. He worked in sub-arctic Canada, in a community-owned BT project. He has served as the ‘executive sponsor’ for A Guide for Planning the Future of Our Language. Currently he serves as overall leader and promoter for Taste\&See and as is SIL liaison with Global Partnerships.
