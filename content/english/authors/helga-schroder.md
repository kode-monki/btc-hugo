Helga Schröder is an Associate Professor at the Department of Linguistics and Languages at the  University of Nairobi, Kenya. Her research area are  Nilotic languages of Sudan and Kenya. She has lately done research on the characteristics of verb-frame versus-satellite framed languages and their challenges in translation.
