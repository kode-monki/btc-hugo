Larry holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from Brown University in Classical Greek, and a Ph.D. in linguistics from the University of Texas, Arlington. He worked as a translation facilitator, and consultant in Indonesia from 1980-1998.  Larry currently is Seed Company’s Senior Vice President, Bible Translation emeritus.  
  
Adriana holds a Bachelor degree from Artha Wacana Christian University Kupang in Theology. She earned a Masters of Theological Studies and a Doctor of Ministry in Hebrew and Old Testament  from Pacific School of Religion. Adriana is a minister of the Protestant Evangelical Church in Timor (GMIT) and a lecturer at Theological Department of Artha Wacana Christian University Kupang.
