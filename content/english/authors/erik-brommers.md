Erik Brommers is a software engineer with SIL Australia, and the developer of the Adapt It Mobile app for iOS and Android. Erik has worked on projects ranging from enterprise-level administration tools to small open-source projects for users who are new to technology. He has a wonderful wife, three great kids, and an amazing recipe for cinnamon rolls.
