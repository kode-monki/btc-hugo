Dr. Catherine Young currently serves as Director for Global Language and Development Services for SIL International. She has  worked in the Philippines and Bangladesh and also served as the Director of LEAD Asia. Currently, Catherine lives in Gloucester, UK. She has a PhD in Education with a specialism in multilingual education policy and planning.
