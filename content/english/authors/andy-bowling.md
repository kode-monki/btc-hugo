Andy taught 7 years in Lebanon learning some market place Arabic. Then during thirty years at John Brown he produced papers for ETS and articles in reference works published by Zondervan, Moody Press, and Baker . Most recently, he was the junior, partner with Robert Longacre in their discourse based analysis of Hebrew grammar.
