<p>Gerhard Marx serves as the Director for the Center for Oral Scriptures, a centre of the Institute of Biblical Languages and Translation.The 4.2.20 Foundation with its academic institution, known as the Institute of Biblical Languages and Translation, is a source language capacity builder for the Bible Translation Movement, providing education and tools to accelerate access to the full Bible for people of all languages, whether written, oral or signed.</p>
