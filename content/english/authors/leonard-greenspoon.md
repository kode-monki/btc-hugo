<p>Leonard Greenspoon holds the Klutznick Chair in Jewish Civilization at Creighton University.  Over several decades he has written and spoken about Bible translations, with  a unique emphasis on Jewish versions.  Recently he published "Jewish Bible Translations: Personalities, Passions, Politics, Progress" with JPS/UNP.</p>
