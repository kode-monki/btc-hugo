Koon Wei is a Deaf person, graduated with MA in Sign Linguistics at Gallaudet University, Washington DC. She has been working with Spring team as a translator since 2013.  
Say Wah is a hearing person, graduated with MA in Arts and Theological Study. She has been working with Peace River team as a resource person since 2011.  
Both of them are currently consultants in training. They have been working with few translation project teams in Asia.
