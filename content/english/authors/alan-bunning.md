<p>Alan Bunning is the Executive Director of the Center for New Testament Restoration. He received a D.Litt. from the Kensington Theological Academy for his work done in textual criticism. Alan previously was an Assistant Professor of Computer Information Systems at Ivy Tech Community College and then was a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at Purdue University before recently retiring.</p>
