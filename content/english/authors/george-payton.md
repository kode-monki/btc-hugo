George Payton served in East Africa leading a single-language project before moving into translation consulting. He has consulted for translation projects throughout eastern and southern Africa. He has also been involved in training mother tongue translators, translation facilitators and consultants. Currently George teaches linguistics and Bible translation courses at the SIL program at Biola University.
