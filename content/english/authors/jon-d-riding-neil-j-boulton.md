Jon Riding and Neil Boulton form the Glossing Technologies Project for United Bible Societies. The project develops language independent NLP systems to assist Bible translators by automatically analysing elements of natural languages. Their previous work includes the ParaTExt Interlinear, key glossing and the PT Concordance Builder.
