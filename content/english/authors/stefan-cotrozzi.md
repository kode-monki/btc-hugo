Stefano Cotrozzi, PhD, is Global Translation Consultant with Biblica (Colorado Springs/USA). He is mainly responsible for projects in East Asia Pacific but is also involved in a few translations in Europe and Africa. He has previously worked with the Société Biblique de Genève (Switzerland), the Institut Perevoda Biblii (Russia) and Raamatunkäännösinstituutti (Finnland).
