Joseph Weinberg is completing an MA in ancient philology in Jerusalem. He has an MA in Jewish studies (distinction) from UCL (London, UK), being awarded the Weitzman prize for Biblical studies and the Rothschild award for Biblical Hebrew, and a BA (distinction) in Theology from LST (London, UK), being awarded the top academic prize and the Biblical Hebrew prize.
