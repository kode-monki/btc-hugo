Sunil Kurien Mathew  has been involving in the Bible Translation ministry for the past twenty-six years. He has been  instrumental in translating the New Testament into the mother tongue  of the Dungra Bhil Tribe in Western India. He is also leading a team in the translation of the Old Testament. He is also functioning as a Translation Consultant and trainer in several Bible Translation projects far and wide of India. He did Master of Theology from the University of Wales and is also an ordained presbyter of the Church of North India.
