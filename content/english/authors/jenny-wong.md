<p>Jenny Wong is currently Lecturer in Modern Languages at the University of Birmingham. She was Assistant Professor at the School of Translation at Hang Seng University of Hong Kong, lecturing in interpreting. Prior to this, she was Assistant Professor at Beijing Normal University – Hong Kong Baptist University, United International College from 2008-2012. She founded the Society for English Learning through Biblical Literature www.selbl.org to promote the study of English idioms through the Bible for non-native English speakers.</p>
