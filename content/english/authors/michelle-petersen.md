Michelle Petersen (MA Language Development, BA French) is an SIL International Arts Consultant. She has worked in literacy and Scripture radio drama across West and Central Africa. She is part of the teaching team that developed Dallas International University’s MA in World Arts and Graduate Certificate in Scripture Engagement. She teaches Scripture Engagement at the Canada Institute of Linguistics, Langley, BC.
