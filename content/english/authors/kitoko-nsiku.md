Edouard Kitoko Nsiku, is a theologian and philosopher from DR Congo. He holds a PhD in Theology and Biblical Languages Studies (University of Kwazulu Natal, South Africa), the Honorary Doctor Degree in Letters (United Graduated College and Seminary, Tennessee, USA), Post-Doctoral Studies in Linguistics (UEM-Mozambique). He is actually a Bible Translation Consultant / Seed Company and Author of books and articles.
