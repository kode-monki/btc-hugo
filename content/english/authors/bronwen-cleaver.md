<p>Bron Cleaver is a Translation CiT working with the minority peoples of Southern Siberia. She has lived in Siberia for over 20 years and is married with two teenage children. She has just begun a PhD on the topic: A design for a performance-based oral Bible translation in the Southern Altai language (Siberia) in the light of their traditional epics.</p>
