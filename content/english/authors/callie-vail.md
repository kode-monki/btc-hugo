Callie Vail has been working with Render since 2014. She has a bachelor’s degree in Linguistics and Spanish from the University of Wisconsin and has taken master’s level courses in Oral Bible Translation and Translation Principles. She serves as an Oral Bible Translation Trainer for Faith Comes By Hearing.
