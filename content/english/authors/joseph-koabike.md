KOABIKE, Bedouma Joseph is from Togo, West Africa. He holds a MA in Bible Translation from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and a MA of Arts, option German Literature from the University of Lomé Togo. He joined SIL Togo-Benin in 1992 for Bible translation. In 2008 he completed the translation of the New Testament in his mother tongue Moba, a language spoken in the north of Togo and Ghana. Since 2009 to date, he works as translation Consultant and translation Coordinator for SIL Togo-Benin. He has mentored many translation consultants of SIL Togo-Benin Branch. He is married to Lim Sok Chin, Literacy consultant for SIL Togo-Benin.
