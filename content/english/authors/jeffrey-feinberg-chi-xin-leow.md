At the Abraham Center, Jeffrey Feinberg PhD introduces Dallas International students to both Hebrew and Greek Scriptures from a Messianic Jewish perspective. He is Text Manager for the Tree of Life Version (TLV), with substantial input on drafting Deuteronomy. Chi Xin Leow of Malaysia wrote his Covenant College BA thesis on "The Year of Release: Deuteronomy 15:1-11 in Honor-Shame Perspective." An Abraham Center internship this year is part of his preparation for continued Biblical Language and Old Testament Studies at the graduate level.
