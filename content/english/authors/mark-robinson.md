<p>Mark Robinson has been engaged in facilitating translation and language development in West Asia with his family for 22 years. He currently serves as a regional service group director for SIL while continuing work with a local team in translation and MLE. He holds an MA in Intercultural Studies from Biola University and just completed his Doctor of Intercultural Studies degree at Fuller Theological Seminary in June 2021.</p>
