<p>JoAnna M. Hoyt is Associate Professor at Dallas International University where she teaches Hebrew. She also serves as Visiting Professor of Hebrew at Dallas Theological Seminary. In addition to teaching, she serves as the assistant OT editor of the Evangelical Exegetical Commentary series and spends her time reading and researching Hebrew linguistics.</p>
