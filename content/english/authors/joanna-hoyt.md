JoAnna Hoyt is an adjunct professor of Hebrew at Dallas International University and a visiting professor at Dallas Theological Seminary. She holds a Ph.D. and a Th.M. in Old Testament from Dallas Theological Seminary. Her research focus is Hebrew Discourse Analysis and she has recently authored a commentary on Amos, Jonah, and Micah.
