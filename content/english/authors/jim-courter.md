Jim has been involved in Bible translation since studying for his Master's at SIL 1981-3. He served as a translation consultant in Europe for 21 years and in Israel for 10 years. In Israel he worked on a PhD on the Sinai Pericope and taught Biblical Hebrew, Greek and Bible translation history and theory at his university.
