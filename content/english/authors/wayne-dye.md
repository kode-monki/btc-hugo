Wayne Dye  (PhD, Fuller) has been a consultant with SIL for 56 years. Wayne and his wife Sally have trained missionaries in many countries in cross-cultural communication, Scripture engagement, and other aspects of missiology. Wayne is currently a DIU Senior Faculty member.  
Ed Beach (MA) is Founder and President of Storyweavers Global which facilitates conversational SE training.  He previously served as a Bible translator and SE Coordinator for SIL Americas.  
Elenore Beach is the developer of the Weavers approach to training for Good News encounters, currently in 16 countries and 60 languages.

Valerie Fernandez Diaz is a graduate of the Languages and Cultural Studies program in Los Andes University and a graduate of SIL’s CILTA program. She is now serving as the Translation Coordinator for Storyweavers Global, and is also a Storyweavers Global training facilitator.
