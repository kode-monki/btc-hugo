Prosper NONGNIDE is from Benin Republic in West Africa. He holds a Master of Divinity degree in translation studies from Africa International University in Kenya. He works with Wycliffe Benin as translation consultant and translation coordinator. He also serves as Global partnership field coordinator of Wycliffe USA for Benin and Togo. He is married to Catherine and they have three children
